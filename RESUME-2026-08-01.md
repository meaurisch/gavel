The elevation brief flags this as a human call ("Ask; don't decide this one
unilaterally"). E2.1 slips a few deliberate repeat pairs (same two diffs,
sides swapped) into a grading session. You can't tell them apart from real
pairs; they're excluded from the ranking; they yield two numbers: your
**self-agreement rate** and **side-swap flip rate**.

## Step 2 — CP2: YOU grade the 42 blind pairs (manual, ~1–2 h total, $0)

Claude must not grade or reveal — this gold standard was purged twice to
keep it pure. Full context: `docs/grading-brief-2026-07-06.md`.

1. Open PowerShell in the repo and run:
   ```
   .\tasks.ps1 Ui
   ```
   Then open **http://localhost:8247** in your browser.
2. **Check the database picker** in the top-right of the nav bar: it must
   say **results.db** (not results-demo.db / results-shakedown.db). If it
   doesn't, switch it.
3. Click **Grade** in the nav. You land on "Pick a contest".
4. Pick the axis **methodology**. In the cells table, find the row
   **cosmos-B · none · claude-haiku-4-5 · claude-code** (15 contenders,
   30 pairs) and click **"Grade myself"**. (Hover any id for details.)
5. Judge each pair with the keyboard — the footer shows the key chips:
   - **A** = left diff wins, **B** = right diff wins, **T** = tie
   - **arrow keys** = navigate between pairs
   - **X** = expand collapsed unchanged regions
   - **O** = toggle Original | A | B three-way view
   - The question per pair: *"which diff would I rather merge?"* The four
     rubric lenses: correctness, minimality, readability, over-engineering.
   - Cost/tokens/time are hidden by design (your 2026-07-05 decision).
6. **Pause anytime.** Close the tab, stop the server, whatever — sessions
   persist. To resume: Grade → the session picker shows your session with
   a progress bar → click it.
7. When ALL pairs in the session are judged, click **"Reveal results →"**,
   read the warning, and click **"Strike the gavel — reveal"**.
   ⚠️ Reveal is **permanent** — the session closes forever, no more
   judgments. Never reveal a half-graded session you meant to continue.
8. Repeat steps 3–7 for the other two cells:
   - axis **methodology** → **moreiter-A · none · claude-sonnet-4-6**
     (9 pairs, ~15–20 min)
   - axis **model** → **cosmos-B · baseline · claude-code** (3 pairs, ~5 min)
9. Partial coverage is fine — judge what your patience allows; cell 1 has
   the highest value per pair. Bradley-Terry needs ≥3 judgments per cell.

Expected: cell 1 ≈ 45–75 min (cosmos-B diffs run 13–55 KB), cell 2
≈ 15–20 min, cell 3 ≈ 5 min.
