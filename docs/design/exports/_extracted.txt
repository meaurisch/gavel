Identity — wordmark, mark, rationale
gavel
The mark is the
gavel mid-swing
: mallet and handle reduced to two rounded bars, tilted at the moment before the strike. One accent square, one glyph, no gradients. Reads at 16px favicon scale.

Name
gavel
— the strike of judgment. Five letters, says exactly what the tool does (you judge, it tallies), and it verbs beautifully:
gavel run
,
pip install gavel
, "gavel it". The wink is built in — you bang a tiny gavel on your own experiments — and it never needs explaining.
Voice
Playfully professional: a tool by and for engineers who tinker with agentic setups. Near-monochrome surfaces keep hours of judging calm; one lively indigo accent, a winking gavel mark, and microcopy that's allowed a dry joke. The playfulness lives in the accent, the mark, and the words — never in extra ornament.
